For someone who runs a quality engineering or quality intelligence practice: shaping offerings, supporting sales, writing bid responses and presenting to clients.

## Capture

- Client pain points in their words (release delays, regression time, defect leakage, cost of testing)
- What was pitched to whom, and which angle landed or fell flat (`pitch`)
- Decision-makers, influencers and what each cares about (`contact` on the person thread, linked to the account)
- Bid facts: client, deadline, scope, evaluation criteria, partners, our win themes
- Commitments: POCs, follow-ups, proposals, demos, with due dates
- Assets produced: decks, case studies, bid answers, demo scripts, and where the file lives
- Win/loss outcomes and the stated reason (`win-loss`)
- Offering changes: new accelerators, pricing models, tool partnerships

## Never store

- Client pricing, rate cards or commercial terms (store "rate card v3 sent, see bid folder")
- Anything under NDA beyond the fact that the conversation happened
- Personal opinions about named client staff
- Candidate personal data from hiring

## Shortcuts

- "Catch me up on <account>" : account thread, last 10 strands, open commitments, linked people with their role, last pitch and how it landed.
- "What did I pitch to <account>?" : all `pitch` strands on the account and its bids, newest first.
- "Who do I know at <account>?" : linked person threads, one line each with role and what they care about.
- "What's due this week?" : `commitment open` strands with due dates in the next 7 days, across all threads.
- "Reusable content for <topic>" : `asset` strands tagged or mentioning the topic, with file locations.
- "Why did we lose <bid>?" : `win-loss` and `risk` strands on the bid thread.
